Move the guides up the rule page and give them real provenance

The modern-web-guidance links sat ninth on a rule page, below the hand-rolled
shapes and the linter note, styled as a plain bullet list. They now sit
directly under the conditions, as cards, above a line naming the owner,
licence, pinned version and the date this project indexed the guide set.

Each card also shows the guide's own retrieve command, which was already in
the catalog data and previously thrown away.
